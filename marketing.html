Built by Captain Joel — 50+ years on the water.

Joel Dubey is a licensed captain who learned to fish growing up in the Florida Keys, and has spent over 50 years chasing fish around the world — from Alaska and Hawaii to Puerto Rico, Mexico, the Bahamas, Belize, Costa Rica, Brazil, Zambia, the Philippines, New Zealand, Australia, Tahiti, Kiribati, Indonesia, the Maldives, the Cook Islands, Panama, and dozens of other waters. He now splits his time between the Bahamas and Florida, still traveling the world in search of new species to catch. That firsthand knowledge is paired with real environmental data — live weather from Open-Meteo and tide predictions from NOAA CO-OPS and WorldTides — so every forecast blends decades on the water with hard numbers.
